\section{The Market Level}

The test period lies after almost all of the labelled data, so carrying the market forward is the hardest part of this task. We therefore treat it before the models of Section~\ref{sec:models}, which describes the model we submit.

\subsection{Validation and Baseline Model}
\label{sec:holdout}

Because the test period lies after the training period, all model comparisons use time-ordered splits evaluated using RMSE. Models are first tuned on a split trained on January 2021 to March 2023 and evaluated on April to December 2023. They are then compared on a holdout period, trained on January 2021 to December 2023 and evaluated on January 2024 to March 2025.

The baseline is a LightGBM model on the 19 features of Section~\ref{sec:dataset}, chosen as described in Section~\ref{sec:lightgbm}. Its settings were chosen on the tuning split from a starting point and 20 random draws: 63 leaves, a minimum of 100 samples per leaf, 357 rounds at a learning rate of 0.05, subsampling of 0.85 of rows and 0.5 of columns, L2 regularisation of 20 and categorical smoothing of 30.
