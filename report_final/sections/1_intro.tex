\section{Introduction}

In Singapore's HDB rental market, shifting prices create much uncertainty for tenants budgeting and flat owners renting out their properties. For instance, in September 2025, rental prices saw an average increase of 0.3\% in mature towns compared with the previous month, whereas non-mature towns saw the opposite, with prices decreasing by 0.6\% during the same period. Furthermore, the rental prices of 3-room flats rose by 0.6\%, yet those for 5-room and Executive flats decreased by 0.4\% and 3\%, respectively \cite{David:2025:Rental}. These contrasting trends highlight some interesting dynamics observed in the rental market, where prices differ based on a plethora of factors.

Given this ever-shifting landscape, our study performs an in-depth analysis of the HDB flat rental market. First, we identify and experiment with potential factors affecting rental price. Afterwards, we build a model that estimates rents for future months from the characteristics of each property and historical trends, at the last observed market level. We then ask whether connections between nearby blocks improve prediction, and examine what drives the predictions.

The contributions of this study benefit multiple stakeholders. Tenants can use rental estimates to assess whether listings are priced reasonably while satisfying budget constraints. Flat owners can better understand the rental market, identify factors that drive their flat's valuation, and make informed pricing decisions that maintain their competitiveness. In particular, these insights may be especially useful for owners renting out their flats for the first time by bridging the information gap. Public agencies and policymakers may also use these findings to understand rental patterns.
